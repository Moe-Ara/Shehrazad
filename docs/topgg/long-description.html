Commands

Type / in any channel to see them. /help lists them by category inside Discord.

▶ Playback

/playPlay a song or playlist by name, link or uploaded file
/playnext: TruePut a song at the front of the queue
/playlistshuffle: TrueQueue a whole playlist or album, optionally shuffled
/pause/resumePause and resume playback
/skipSkip, or vote to skip, the current track
/previousGo back to the previous track
/seektime: 1:30Jump to a time in the track
/volumelevel: 80Show or set the volume (1–150%)
/loopRepeat off, the track, or the whole queue
/nowplayingShow the current track and the player panel
/join/leaveJoin or leave your voice channel
/stopStop, clear the queue and leave

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask most.

The slash commands don't show upPress Ctrl+R on desktop, or close and reopen Discord on your phone. Discord sometimes needs a refresh after a bot is added.
Can it play Spotify links?Not directly, because Spotify doesn't allow streaming to bots. Search by song name instead: /play song name.
Does it need admin?No. It only asks for the six permissions above. /setup tells you if a channel blocks it.
What data is stored?Server settings, recently played tracks (kept 30 days), and your liked tracks and saved playlists. Never voice audio. Use /privacy to export or delete it.
