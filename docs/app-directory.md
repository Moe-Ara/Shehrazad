# App Directory listing (draft copy)

**Name:** Shehrazad
**Short description (≤ 200 chars):** A clean, reliable music bot: search or paste a link, control
everything from one player panel. Queue tools, vote-skip, DJ roles — free. 24/7 and saved playlists with Premium.

**Tags:** Music, Utility, Social

**Long description**

Shehrazad plays music in your voice channel with one tidy, always-up-to-date player panel instead of
a flood of messages.

- 🎵 `/play` a song name, a YouTube or SoundCloud link, a playlist, or upload an audio file
- 🎛️ One player panel: pause, skip, back, repeat, shuffle, volume, queue and ❤️ like
- 📜 Real queue tools: move, remove, jump, shuffle, clear, seek, previous
- 🗳️ Vote-skip, DJ role and DJ mode so the room stays in control
- ⚙️ `/setup` in under a minute, with a built-in permission check
- 🎀 `/anime` for cute, safe-for-work anime pictures and reaction GIFs
- 🔒 Minimal permissions, no message reading, `/privacy` to export or delete your data

**Premium (per server):** saved server playlists, 24/7 mode, queue restore after restarts, bigger queues
and playlist imports, longer idle timeout, priority when we're busy. Playback and controls stay free.

**Permissions requested and why**
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links | Post the player panel and replies |
| Read Message History | Edit and tidy up the player panel |
| Connect, Speak | Play audio in voice |
