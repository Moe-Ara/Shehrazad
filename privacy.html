Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Shehrazad stores as little as possible: your server's settings, what was played recently, and things you choose to save. It can't read your messages, never records voice, and never sees your email or payment details.

You can export or delete your data at any time with the /privacy command.

1. Who is responsible

Shehrazad is run by its developer, Moe, who is responsible for the data described here. You can reach us with the bot's /feedback command or on GitHub.

While music is playing, the queue and voice state are also held in memory. What you type into /play is used to find the track and isn't stored; only the track that plays shows up in the history.

3. What we don't collect

  • Message content. The bot has no access to your messages; it only works through slash commands and buttons.
  • Voice audio. The bot only sends audio and never records anything.
  • Email addresses, IP addresses or payment details. Discord handles all payments.
  • Profile information beyond your Discord user ID. We don't sell data, show ads or track you.

4. Legal bases

If you are in the EU or UK, we process data:

  • to provide the service you asked for, such as playing music, saving favorites and Premium (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR);
  • for our legitimate interest in keeping the bot secure, preventing abuse and fixing problems, through logs and rate limits (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Who else is involved

  • Discord runs the platform the bot works on and handles all payments for Premium. See Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Sparked Host LLC runs the server the bot lives on, in Miami, Florida, USA. The data in section 2 is therefore stored and processed in the United States, which may not offer the same level of data protection as the EU.
  • YouTube and SoundCloud: when you play something, our server sends your search text or link to them to find and stream the track. No user IDs are sent.
  • nekos.best: /anime only sends the picture category, such as "hug". No user data is sent.
  • Top.gg may receive the total number of servers the bot is in. That number contains no personal data.
  • GitHub hosts this website, and processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver and protect it. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. The quickest way is the /privacy command in Discord:

  • Export my data sends you a file with everything stored about you.
  • Delete my data deletes your favorites and feedback, and removes your user ID from the play history and saved playlists.
  • Server admins can also export or delete all of their server's data.

For anything else, contact us (see below). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

7. Children

Shehrazad is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age. If you think we hold data about a child who shouldn't be using Discord, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and announce significant changes in advance.

9. Contact

Use the bot's /feedback command, or open an issue on GitHub.
